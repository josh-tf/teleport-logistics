# Relay 0.4.2 item connector repair

The previous release closed the far rear but intentionally left a lateral bay
between the native conveyor collar and the rear pylons. The in-game screenshot
showed that opening was still unwanted. Both item variants now have continuous
side walls and a floor joining the collar shoulders to the existing rear bulkhead.
The circular field remains inside, visible through the open belt mouth.

The positive-Y vent array was centered 16 cm beyond the panel face and extended
past its width. It is now a smaller array embedded in the panel. The panel bolts
are low-profile hexagonal heads seated in its surface.

The connector transforms, native collar rim geometry, paint atlas, material sections,
and runtime behavior are unchanged. Camera direction is now configurable in the
model capture script so the opposite side and rear can be reviewed explicitly.

Validation results are recorded in 0.4.2-validation.txt. The new exported-mesh
check samples both side walls and the rear, checks that the belt mouth remains
open, and rejects vent geometry outside the panel. Running it against the released
0.4.1 meshes fails on the open side wall. Shipping-game visual acceptance remains
pending; studio renders are an approximation of game lighting and materials.

Direct entrance inspection also exposed the stock collar's flat black terminal
plate, which hid the circular field from the front. That plate is removed in both
item variants. The circular field and far bulkhead now terminate the open throat;
checks extend through the entire native mouth, not only the custom housing.

The offline interactive viewer includes all five actual models, paint and light
previews, wireframe, native connection markers and eight camera presets. Browser
checks run with external networking blocked. The displayed source triangle counts
are checked to prevent loose OBJ edges from being mistaken for the entire mesh.
