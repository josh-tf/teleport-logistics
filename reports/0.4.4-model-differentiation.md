# Relay 0.4.4: distinguish input and output models

The four endpoints now differ in physical shape as well as their direction marks. The change keeps the existing industrial family, connector locations, closed housings and native paint materials.

| Model | Input | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Item | One broad roof intake cowl, flat side cover and single rear service panel | Two sloping roof rails, paired side modules and two rear service panels |
| Fluid | Broad low cap and single rear service cover | Raised smaller cap and two rear pressure canisters |

The pattern is deliberately modest: one broad intake housing versus paired delivery hardware. It remains visible when players give both variants the same Customizer paint. The circular item field and unobstructed conveyor mouth are retained. The hub retains its shape and receives the same new identification plate styling.

Every identification plate now carries a flush FICSIT mark and Open Sans Bold lettering from the game. Labels spell out `ITEM RELAY (Input)`, `ITEM RELAY (Output)`, `FLUID RELAY (Input)`, `FLUID RELAY (Output)` and `RELAY HUB`. The item plate was raised after a close-up revealed its lettering touching the screen screws. The bundled logo outline and font include separate attribution and license notices.

Fasteners were reseated on the two different fluid cap heights during the geometry review. New parts share the existing consolidated material sections; there are no added actors, lights, material slots or runtime ticks. The family increases from 133,350 to 139,990 triangles (6,640 triangles, approximately 5.0%). All five models retain one stable LOD.

Front and rear studio captures are in `reports/model-renders/0.4.4/`. The offline model viewer embeds the actual regenerated meshes and supports orbiting every model, paint, clay and wireframe previews. These are source-mesh previews; native Satisfactory lighting and shader appearance still require an in-game check.

Validation and packaging results are recorded separately in `0.4.4-validation.txt`.
