# Relay 0.3.3 model refinement review

The pass replaces the disconnected, extruded RELAY / IN / OUT / NETWORK lettering with compact surface markings attached to real console bezels and the hub side rail. It retains the custom hub mast, machine dimensions, native connection hardware, closed fluid reducer, and item collar rear housing.

## Reference comparison

Compared the current generated models and the supplied in-game screenshot against the vendored stock-style common parts and their authored UV/material sections. These are community reconstructions of standard Satisfactory parts, not ripped game assets or a full stock-machine export. Sources: [Satisfactory Modeling Tools common parts](https://github.com/DavidHGillen/Satisfactory_ModelingTools/blob/master/SF_CommonParts.md), [material/decals guidance](https://docs.ficsit.app/satisfactory-modding/latest/Development/Modeling/MainMaterials.html), and the local upstream Blender files. The existing native collar flow/caution decals remain intact.

| Area | Before | Refinement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identification | Thick freestanding letters with no backing, sometimes visibly suspended above the machine | Flat, non-emissive dark stencil lettering on a small aluminum service plate |
| Placement | Labels compete with the native collar flow graphics | Item/fluid plate belongs to the console bezel; hub plate belongs to its side rail |
| Visual hierarchy | Warning-yellow bolts scattered around the frames and feet | Ordinary fasteners use steel; functional warnings and native connector direction graphics keep their emphasis |
| Material integration | Custom geometry already uses the native factory atlas | Same runtime slots, atlas mapping, Customizer paint regions and lamp separation retained |
| Shape and connection geometry | Custom machine bodies around stock-scale connection hardware | Body silhouettes, custom mast, belt/pipe snap locations and rear closures preserved |

The lettering is a **surface-applied mesh decal**, not a deferred projection or a newly added bitmap texture. Glyphs have zero extrusion and bevel, sit 0.08 cm above their supporting plate, and use existing factory-atlas material sections. This avoids introducing another shader, masked section, or text texture. The generator asserts that glyphs are planar and fit within their host plates. Native connector decals continue to use the existing native decal materials.

## Review passes

1. Replaced floating text, added integrated label support, generated models, and inspected a fluid-output studio render. The placement was sound; white-on-dark lettering was too subdued in the studio lighting.
2. Switched to dark lettering on aluminum plates and normal steel fasteners; regenerated all models and descriptor/milestone captures. Inspected the revised fluid-output render: the plate reads as an attached service label and the ordinary bolts no longer compete with flow indicators.
3. Applied the exact requested naming: ITEM RELAY (IN), ITEM RELAY (OUT), FLUID RELAY (IN), FLUID RELAY (OUT), and RELAY HUB. Endpoint plates use two lines; the bezel was extended just enough to support the taller plate. Regenerated all five models and their captures again, and inspected the final fluid-output and hub captures for wording, support, and custom mast retention.
4. The hub review exposed another existing unsupported-looking detail: its screens tilted against the console slope. Corrected the screen housing angle and height from the actual shell top plane, and regenerated only the hub mesh, descriptor/milestone captures, front/rear and unpowered renders. The top falls from Z176 to Z133.08 over 145 cm in X (gradient −0.296), requiring +16.489° Blender Y rotation. All four bottom corners of both display housings are asserted to sit 1 cm inside the shell plane.

The first-pass render is retained as `reports/model-renders/SM_RelayFluidOutput-art-pass1.png`. The final normal model renders and descriptor captures use the revised materials. Studio previews use the community atlas proxy; the game supplies the actual native factory/decal shaders. These renders do not establish in-game material or multiplayer acceptance.

## Validation

- `python3 scripts/generate-assets.py`: passed; five OBJ/MTL models, model capture pairs, map markers, category icon, and milestone images regenerated together.
- `python3 scripts/validate-model-alignment.py`: passed. All original belt/pipe connector vertices remain at the documented transforms; compact power geometry retains 99.9% after cleanup/rounding. The custom mast top-cap and C++ cable-anchor check passes. All 192 fluid-annulus closure rays pass.
- `python3 scripts/validate.py`: passed. All mesh triangle/UV/normal references, dimensions, material sections, image families, retained package definitions and power-aware material configuration pass.
- Final family: **107,764 triangles**. The pre-label-refinement family was approximately 132,191 triangles, so this pass removes about 18.5%. Compared directly with the released `dist/Relay-0.3.2-source.zip` model sources (127,095 triangles), the complete revised family is 15.2% smaller despite adding the closed connector housings.
- The descriptor/milestone captures use the final source meshes and show the requested wording. No new runtime material slots or custom surface textures were added.

All five full-size front renders and the hub/item-output/fluid-output rear renders have been inspected after the final naming pass. Nameplates have physical support, the text is flat and inset within the plate boundaries, and the closed connector backs and custom mast remain intact. The final corrected hub front, rear and unpowered renders are inspected: both display housings follow the shell plane; lamp, beacon and screen emission is disabled in the unpowered preview. The overview, descriptor-capture and hub power-state sheets are refreshed with exact requested names.

The hub display seating issue found during the review is corrected in the final geometry. Mast, connector transforms and label placement remain unchanged by that correction.
